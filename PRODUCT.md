# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Ateneo Celadon project managers (students running org projects) and the
EBCB who supervise them. They open the toolkit mid-task on a phone or laptop
to answer a specific question: how to submit a PPF, book a venue, get
reimbursed, request an email blast, file a leave of absence.

## Purpose

The single reference for how a Celadon project runs: procedures, every
department's requirements of PMs, and who to contact. It replaces the
2026–2027 Google Site at pm.ateneoceladon.com.

## Constraints

- Public static site on GitHub Pages. No personal phone numbers, passcodes,
  or tax IDs in content. Internal Google files stay behind Google sharing.
- Content is edited by EBCB members in Markdown, often from the GitHub web
  editor. Structure must stay simple to edit.
- Brand is inherited from ateneoceladon.com (Celadon Website Brandbook).
- Rules the departments haven't settled are shown as "Needs EBCB
  confirmation" rather than decided by the site.
