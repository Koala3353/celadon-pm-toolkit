Fix these first

Each one is under 15 minutes in the Sites editor and stops a PM from being sent the wrong way.

  1. Swap the content of Reward Practices and Conflict Resolution. Each page shows the other's text.
  2. Fix the PPF template link on the FIN guide. It returns "You need access" for a student account, and the page calls it the most important document.
  3. Repoint the five COMMPUB EBCB email links. Four of them open an email to Jillian Yu, and the domain on that page doesn't match the EBCB Directory.
  4. Unlink about 30 paragraphs on the COMMPUB page. Whole sentences open an email, the logo folder or the DRF when clicked.
  5. Swap the "Pre-Project" and "Post-Project" headings in the OSR checklist. Each heading currently sits on the other's list.
  6. Replace the closed CFMO form links with a link to the CFMO Forms page, so the toolkit stops sending PMs to closed forms.
  7. Pick one rule each for receipts (invoice or OR), reimbursement time (3 weeks or 2 months) and when to reserve CFMO venues.
  8. Settle when an LOA or resignation takes effect and whether it gets announced. The HR page gives two or three answers for each.

What's working

  • The site's structure is clear: one guide per department, a consistent card grid on Home and Guides, and a directory with every EBCB member's email and Facebook.
  • Nearly all linked files are current-year (2627 / '26–'27) and open for members.
  • COMMPUB's approval flow is specific and checkable: 3-day IDL, 12-hour comment window, 10-day assignment lead time.
  • The 2026 caterer and food-entity lists are already in place.
  • OSR's request processes (data, email blasts, FUSION) have lead times and named contact people.

Not covered

  • Canva links, Google Calendar embed and booking page, CFMO Zoom room, OSA portal short links and social links were not opened.
  • No visual pass for colour contrast or mobile layout. The findings come from page content and structure.
  • Site sharing settings could not be viewed, so it's unconfirmed whether the site is open to all of student.ateneo.edu or only Celadon.
  • Text that exists only inside images (for example the receipt-type table on FIN and the CTA timeline on OSR) was not checked.
