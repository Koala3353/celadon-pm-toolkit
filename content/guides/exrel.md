## Centralized Sponsorship Repository (CSR)

\* Prior to the finalization of a partnership, approval of the ExRel EBCB is required.

**Purpose**

- The Centralized Sponsorship Repository is a new and improved system that aims to collate partnerships data across all projects for transparency of information and ease of reference both within the present Academic Year and future terms.
- **Why?** This stems from a lack of data retention on project partnerships across the previous several years, leading to loss of potential sponsorships and a lack of sustainability in maintaining meaningful relations with partners.

**How it Works**

- The CSR will be visible to everyone, made available for the use of EBCB, Managers, and ExRel Core Teams.
  - However, edit access will only be available to the ExRel EBCB.
- Access the [Centralized Sponsorship Repository](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1tsHUFbd0Zq4q7x7w-kyrg2Rz7A7SQSBYNA96C7lwais/edit) here.
  - The Landing Page reflects the respective ExRel Partnerships Masterfiles of each Project, as well as vital information and a summary of the status of each project's contacting phase.
  - The subsequent tabs are labeled and linked to corresponding ExRel Partnerships Masterfiles per project. They specifically reflect the 'Preliminary List of Partners' sheet.
- **ExRel Core Teams and/or their PM's are expected to update the 'Preliminary List of Partners' sheet** in their Partnerships Masterfiles on a weekly basis at minimum.
  - The fields in the 'Preliminary List of Partners' sheet are:
    - Company Name
    - Email
    - Point Person (from Project CT)
    - Package Type (for projects with multiple)
    - Benefits & Responsibilities (list tier)
    - Status (Replied, Negotiating)
    - MOA (provide the link and tag your assigned ExRel EBCB for approval)
    - Justification (only applicable for ex-deals, explain what use the products will be to the project)
    - Others

## Deployment: EXREL Core Team Request Process

- Consultation with the EXREL EBCB should be done first to have the permission of getting partnerships and/or sponsorships core teams.

### EXREL Committee

The necessity of having an EXREL team be deployed for the project would ONLY be finalized through deliberations among the Project Managers and EBCB involved.

*\*Note: Number of Core Team Members to be deployed depends on the available Core Managers and Associates within the department.*

### EXREL Core Manager

- Only **ONE (1)** Core Manager will be assigned to each EXREL core team of the project
  - Requests for additional Core Managers are subject for approval and needs to IC with any EXREL EBCB
- Requests for certain EXREL Core Managers to be deployed to their project will be considered but will eventually depend on the EXREL EBCB's deliberation based on the manager's availability and project interests

### EXREL Associates

- Each project is expected to allocate **two (2) to three (3) slots for EXREL Associates**.
- Beyond that the number of Associates that will be deployed to each EXREL core team will depend on the scale and needs of the project.
  - Requests for additional Associates are subject for approval and deliberations by the EXREL EBCB
- Requests for certain EXREL Associates to be deployed to their project will be considered but will eventually depend on the EXREL EBCB's deliberation based on the associate's availability and project interests
- ONLY EXREL department members and accepted EXREL CTA members shall accomplish EXREL-specific tasks, including but not limited to:
  - Creation and Drafting of Marketing Packages
  - Memorandum of Agreement
  - Representation in external meetings
  - Accommodating the receipt and storage of ex-deal products

*\*Note: All details inputted are subject to change and finalization upon consultation with the EXREL EBCB.*

*\*Note: For Department Initiatives that require Partnerships & Sponsorships, kindly contact the EXREL EBCB or Partnerships Managers directly.*

## EXREL Alumni Request Consultation

Project Managers that require alumni engagement (event speakers, alumni tie ups, etc.) must set up a consultation with the EXREL EBCB. Please prepare the following for the consultation:

- How many alumni would you be needing?
- Why do you need alumni engagement?
- When will you be needing alumni engagement?
- What will you be providing these alumni? (tokens, payments, etc.)
- Who do you wish to get in touch with? (if applicable)

*\*Note:* Alumni information may only be released subject to the approval of the EXREL EBCB.

## Workflow of Partnership Acquisition

:::steps
1. **Pre-contacting Phase**
   1. Project Managers create the timeline for contacting and securing partners.
   2. The ExRel Core Team drafts the [Marketing Package](https://canva.link/9ijoeuu1gw85j7f), using the provided template and customizing according to project needs or at special request by the PM's.
2. **Contacting Phase:**
   1. ExRel Core Head distributes the contacting of sponsors within the ExRel Core Team
   2. Mailmeteor is utilized for automated, mass dissemination of e-mails.
3. **CSR:** Once a sponsor replies, the ExRel Core Team (particularly, the specific point person) must update their 'Preliminary List of Partners'.
   1. If this is an ex-deal offer, they must fill out the "Justification" field, addressing the following:
      1. Potential partner's relevance to the project, particularly in terms of brand alignment
      2. Ex-deal's purpose in the project (e.g. giveaway, prize)
4. **MOA Drafting:** The ExRel Core Team/Point Person is responsible for drafting a MOA enveloping all benefits and responsibilities agreed upon by both parties.
5. **MOA Signing**: These details will undergo review by the ExRel EBCB, then be forwarded to the Organization Moderator via the [OSA Portal](https://sites.google.com/ateneo.edu/ateneolsosa/home)
   1. MOAs may only be signed by the ExRel EBCB
   2. MOAs will be submitted by ExRel Core Teams through the CSR, wherein they are expected to tag the email account of the assigned EBCB to request approval.
6. **Compliance with Responsibilities**
   1. The ExRel Core Team is expected to comply with the responsibilities outlined in the Memorandum of Agreement, and document compliance through the Compliance Tracker.
:::

## Marketing Package

1. **Type**
   1. Concessionaire
      1. For bazaars (LLM, CNY), pricing typically varies depending on the scale of the event and the nature of the concessionaire
   2. Sponsorship
      1. Typically 3 to 4 tiers indicating different values and their corresponding benefits
      2. Can be in form of full cash, partial cash and partial ex-deal, or full ex-deal
   3. Media Partnership
      1. Primarily digital benefits
      2. Often in form of agreement with other student organizations or media organizations (e.g. ChinoyTV, ChinatownTV)
2. **Content**
   1. Title Page
   2. About Ateneo Celadon
   3. Project Description
   4. Why Partner with Us?
      1. Information on Social Media Reach
      2. Information on Previous Partner/s
      3. Other Contextual Information
   5. Responsibilities and Benefits
   6. Contact Information
      1. PMs/ExRel Heads
