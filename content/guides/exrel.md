## Centralized Sponsorship Repository (CSR)

:::note
The ExRel EBCB must approve every partnership before it's finalized.
:::

**Purpose.** The CSR collects partnership data from every project in one place, so it's transparent and easy to look up this year and in future terms. It exists because partnership records were lost over several years, which cost the org potential sponsorships and made it hard to keep good relationships with partners.

**How it works**

- Everyone can view the CSR: EBCB, Managers, and ExRel core teams. Only the ExRel EBCB can edit it.
- The **landing page** shows each project's ExRel Partnerships Masterfile, key information, and a summary of each project's contacting status.
- The **following tabs** link to each project's Partnerships Masterfile and mirror its *Preliminary List of Partners* sheet.
- **ExRel core teams or their PMs update the Preliminary List of Partners at least once a week.**

**Fields in the Preliminary List of Partners**

:::columns
- Company name
- Email
- Point person (from the project core team)
- Package type (for projects with more than one)
- Benefits and responsibilities (list the tier)
- Status (Replied, Negotiating)
- MOA (link it and tag your assigned ExRel EBCB for approval)
- Justification (ex-deals only: explain how the products will be used in the project)
- Others
:::

## Deploying an EXREL core team

Consult the EXREL EBCB first to get permission for a partnerships or sponsorships core team. Whether a project gets an EXREL team is decided only through deliberation between the Project Managers and the EBCB involved.

:::compare
**EXREL Core Manager**

- Each project's EXREL core team gets **one** Core Manager.
- Requests for more Core Managers need approval and an IC with any EXREL EBCB.
- You can request specific Core Managers, but the EXREL EBCB decides based on availability and project interest.

**EXREL Associates**

- Each project should allot **2 to 3 slots** for EXREL Associates.
- Beyond that, the number deployed depends on the project's scale and needs.
- Requests for more Associates, or for specific ones, are decided by the EXREL EBCB based on availability and project interest.
:::

Only EXREL department members and accepted EXREL CTA members do EXREL-specific tasks, including:

- Creating and drafting marketing packages
- Memoranda of agreement
- Representing the project in external meetings
- Receiving and storing ex-deal products

:::note
Deployment numbers depend on the Core Managers and Associates available in the department, and every detail is subject to change after consultation with the EXREL EBCB. For department initiatives that need partnerships and sponsorships, contact the EXREL EBCB or the Partnerships Managers directly.
:::

## Alumni requests

Project Managers who need alumni engagement, such as event speakers or alumni tie-ups, must set up a consultation with the EXREL EBCB. Come prepared to answer:

- How many alumni do you need?
- Why do you need alumni engagement?
- When do you need it?
- What will you give the alumni (tokens, payments, and so on)?
- Who do you want to reach, if anyone specific?

:::note
Alumni information is released only with the EXREL EBCB's approval.
:::

## How partnerships are won

:::steps
1. **Pre-contacting.** Project Managers set the timeline for contacting and securing partners. The ExRel core team drafts the [marketing package](https://canva.link/9ijoeuu1gw85j7f) from the template, customized for the project or at the PMs' request.
2. **Contacting.** The ExRel core head divides the sponsor list within the core team. Mass emails go out through Mailmeteor.
3. **Update the CSR.** When a sponsor replies, the point person updates the Preliminary List of Partners. For ex-deal offers, fill in the Justification field: how the partner fits the project and its brand, and what the ex-deal is for (a giveaway, a prize).
4. **Draft the MOA.** The ExRel core team or point person drafts an MOA covering every benefit and responsibility both parties agreed to.
5. **Sign the MOA.** The ExRel EBCB reviews it, then it goes to the org Moderator through the [OSA Portal](https://sites.google.com/ateneo.edu/ateneolsosa/home). Only the ExRel EBCB may sign MOAs. Core teams submit MOAs through the CSR and tag their assigned EBCB's email to request approval.
6. **Deliver on the MOA.** The ExRel core team fulfils the responsibilities in the MOA and records them in the Compliance Tracker.
:::

## Marketing packages

### Types

:::columns
**Concessionaire**

- For bazaars (LLM, CNY). Pricing usually depends on the scale of the event and the type of concessionaire.

**Sponsorship**

- Usually 3 to 4 tiers, each with its value and benefits.
- Full cash, part cash and part ex-deal, or full ex-deal.

**Media partnership**

- Mostly digital benefits.
- Often with other student organizations or media outlets, such as ChinoyTV and ChinatownTV.
:::

### What goes in a marketing package

1. Title page
2. About Ateneo Celadon
3. Project description
4. Why partner with us: social media reach, previous partners, and other context
5. Responsibilities and benefits
6. Contact information for the PMs and ExRel heads
