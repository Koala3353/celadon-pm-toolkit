## Core team management

### Core head roles and responsibilities

1. Serve as the point person for both COMMPUB and the Project Managers in their assigned project core team.
2. Lead their core team in delegating, planning, and executing tasks properly and on time.
3. Make sure every output from their core team meets the standards and portrays the project appropriately.
4. Work with their core team to conceptualize ideas and how to execute them.
5. Help their core team members carry out their tasks.

### Deployment

- COMMPUB staffers may be deployed into the Digital Creatives (DC), Documentation and Publications (DocPub), and Production Design (PD) committees.
- Project Managers should leave room for COMMPUB deployment in their core teams. As a working estimate, allot 1–2 slots for each COMMPUB pool involved: 1–2 DC staffers for the DC committee, 1–2 Production Design staffers for the PD committee, and 1 Videos, 1 Photos, and 1 Writing staffer for the DocPub committee.
- Deployed COMMPUB staffers skip the regular project application process, as long as their pool matches the committee they join. Once deployed, they are part of the core team.

- For cross-committee work, create a dedicated chat when needed, such as Photos × Production Design for DP shoots, or Photos × Digital Creatives for publications that involve photoshoots. Use it to align task kickoff, comments, and approval routing.
- The COMMPUB EBCB point person will post the important links in each committee's chat. Pin that message: the DRF, Brandbook, Cheat Sheet, Spiel Stylebook, Spiel Masterfile, Celadon Posting Calendar, and the Project Masterfile if there is one.

**What COMMPUB Managers do in the group chats**

1. Monitor the progress of the COMMPUB core teams.
2. Answer the project's questions on COMMPUB matters.
3. Monitor the quality of the core teams' outputs.
4. Help the core teams plan and execute their outputs.
5. Give comments and approval when the core team asks.
6. Guide core team members, and their heads if needed.
7. Tag the COMMPUB EBCB representative when outputs are approved and ready to post.

### Onboarding core heads and the core team

Project Managers and core heads should orient their core teams using this toolkit. Explain everyone's roles and make sure the team shares the project's creative vision. Show and explain:

- The finalized brandbook
- The Deliverable Request Form
- The Spiel Masterfile
- The [approval process](#approval-system)
- The cheat sheets

You can ask the COMMPUB EBCB or COMMPUB Managers for help when orienting core team members.

## The Deliverable Request Form

### Before the school year starts

:::steps
1. **Draft the brandbook before you finalize the deliverables list in the DRF.** Open the [Brandbook Template](https://canva.link/lrz696trm3lkker) from the link in the DRF and follow the instructions in the Canva file. Message the COMMPUB EBCB if you need help.

   The Project Manager must complete these **required** slides before the school year starts:
   - **Mood board**
   - **Word bank** with at least five words that describe or relate to the project theme. These help Writing staffers set the tone of the spiels and inspire the rest of the core team when planning deliverables.
   - **Deliverables**: the proposed deliverables with pegs and references. The Deliverables Guide in the DRF lists common deliverable types per project.

   **Optional** slides: typography and color palette.
2. **Fill out the [Deliverable Request Form](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-myQOjynb_T34ezsn3F3IvdB49ORsINVvegFGLWbhYg/edit).** Go to the tab named after your project. If it doesn't exist yet, duplicate the template tab and customize the copy. Fill out the Project/Initiative Details section, and read the reminders before filling in the rest.
3. **Copy the brandbook's deliverables into the DRF.** Fill in the Committee, Type, Target Blast Date, and Platform columns for each deliverable, naming the exact platform or posting channel. If dates aren't final, ask the COMMPUB EBCB for recommended ranges or approximate posting weeks.
4. **Schedule the brandbook and DRF meeting with the COMMPUB EBCB.** When your first drafts are done, notify at least one COMMPUB EBCB (tag them in your project's DRF tab, message them on Messenger, or tag them in the Manager Community Chat) so they can review before the consultation. Then [book a slot on the COMMPUB EBCB appointment calendar](https://calendar.app.google/Dsgs6BqJbVvcawzLA). Aim to meet **before the end of July**. Schedule follow-ups only if typography, color palette, or deliverable pegs still need finalizing.
:::

:::note
COMMPUB EBCB and Manager contact details are also on the Welcome page of the DRF.
:::

## Spiel Masterfile

The [Spiel Masterfile](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1dg9EFX2ThorecILJwuhoENXPOg6zIzuyXpD1VyE_Jf8/edit) is where every post caption is written. Like the DRF, each project has its own tab.

- Every social media output in the DRF needs a matching entry in the Spiel Masterfile, filled in by the Project Manager or core head.
- DocPub members assigned to writing put their spiels here, and the Writing Managers comment and approve here.

## Approval system

### General approval flow

:::flow
- Core team member
- Core heads
- Project Managers
- COMMPUB Manager(s) of the pool
- COMMPUB EBCB
:::

- Project Managers and core heads may comment, but **final approval comes from the relevant COMMPUB Manager(s)**.
- The COMMPUB EBCB mostly checks readability, accuracy, and final details before posting.

1. COMMPUB Managers give feedback and approval on every output the project intends to post.
2. Send first drafts for approval **at least 3 days before** the proposed blast date, and tag the managers needed. For a September 15 blast, the first draft goes to the COMMPUB Managers by September 12. That date is the internal deadline (IDL).
3. Once tagged for comments, COMMPUB Managers generally give initial feedback **within 12 hours**. If they can't, they post a transparency notice with the soonest time they can respond.
4. When an output is approved, the COMMPUB Manager marks it **Approved and Ready for Posting** in the DRF or Spiel Masterfile and tags the COMMPUB EBCB representative. Outputs not approved by the relevant COMMPUB Manager(s) and the COMMPUB EBCB will not be posted.

### Instructions by pool

::::details{title="Digital Creatives"}
- Core heads notify the assigned staffer **at least 10 days before the IDL**. Tag them in the Digital Creatives chat. Updating the Assigned column in the DRF alone is not enough.
- Staffers submit drafts by linking the Canva file in the DRF's Link column as a *suggestion*. Designs not made in Canva go on a platform that keeps image quality, such as Google Drive or Imgur, with the link in the same column.
- Staffers then tag the Digital Creatives Managers in the chat to say the pub is ready for comments.
- Managers comment in the chat, the DRF, Canva, or by editing the Canva file directly, within about 12 hours of being tagged. If they can't, they post a transparency notice with the soonest time they can.
- Project Managers and core heads may comment too, but final approval comes from the Digital Creatives Managers.
- If the second Digital Creatives Manager doesn't respond within 12 hours, only the first manager's comments and approval are required. Still consider the second manager's comments if they're crucial.
- This repeats until the output is fully approved. The managers announce the approval in the chat, mark it **Approved and Ready for Posting** in the DRF, and tag the COMMPUB EBCB representative.
- Every publication material must carry the official Celadon logos, the project logo, and sponsor logos. Celadon logos are in [[01] Assets and Logos](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1qovwU_NijCy7LIZXAmamIFttHJBF-zsA).

:::note
Digital Creatives also works with the Photos committee on photo-related outputs such as promotional materials and DP shoot frames. Core heads lead the coordination.
:::
::::

::::details{title="Photos"}
- Event photos and other project photos (such as DP shoot photos) go in a folder or album in the DocPub folder, inside the COMMPUB folder of the project's Google Drive.
- Put photo album links in the DRF's Link column for the relevant deliverable, such as DP Shoot or Event Documentation Album.

**Onsite event documentation**

- Photographers edit their own photos and may submit up to **100 photos per day** they cover.
- Once Digital Creatives finalizes the watermark, send the Canva link to the DocPub chat.
- Each photographer duplicates the watermark template page, adds their name, and saves the watermark as an image.
- Export edited photos with your watermark and upload them to the photo album folder. Ideally, each photographer makes a folder labelled with their name.
- When everything is uploaded, the DocPub core head tags the Photos Managers in the DocPub chat for sorting and approval.
- The Photos Managers review, filter, and sort photos for the final album. When it's approved, they notify the core team and tag the COMMPUB EBCB representative.

**DP shoots**

- The DocPub Photos team runs DP shoots with the Production Design committee; the core heads lead the coordination.
  - Production Design builds the set used for the DP blast.
- Digital Creatives designs the DP frames for both shoot participants and non-participants. The core heads coordinate the two committees so the vision stays aligned.
- DP shoots need venue reservations, arranged early with the Logistics committee, for both the test shoot and the actual shoot. Ideally use the same venue for both.
  - Common venues: Dela Costa consultation rooms, MVP 321 (Music Room).
- Borrow lights and tripods from the COMMPUB EBCB through the [Equipment Logbook Form](#equipment-borrowing).
- Run the test shoot, asking the Photos Managers or COMMPUB EBCB for help if needed. Send sample photos to the Photos Managers by tagging them in the DocPub chat on Messenger for feedback and approval.
- DocPub core heads copy the [DP Shoot Sign-ups Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1IPRcRJ9loKfcmyxvLPqgQv6l5LJ2X2PvMVHl5zoYjGk/edit) into the project's Google Drive and fill it out completely. Tag the COMMPUB EBCB representative in the DocPub chat with the link so they can post it in the internal Celadon Facebook group.
- During the shoot, each member picks 1–2 photos to be post-processed. List the file numbers in the sign-ups sheet.
- After the shoot, post-process the photos with your core team using the DP frame from Digital Creatives. The Photos Managers can help with feedback while you edit.
- Download the finished photos into a folder in the project's Google Drive. Send the link and tag the Photos Managers in the DocPub chat for final approval.
- Once approved, tag the COMMPUB EBCB representative to say the DP photos are ready. The COMMPUB EBCB posts the DP blast announcement.

**Publication materials that use photos**

- Main posters and other photo-based outputs are made with the Digital Creatives committee. The core heads lead the coordination.
- Ask the Photos Managers or the COMMPUB EBCB for help whenever needed.

::::details{title="Writing"}

:::steps

## Posting on social media

### Celadon Posting Calendar

- The [Celadon Posting Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed), also linked in the DRF, tracks every upcoming post across all Celadon projects and social media channels.
- Anyone can view it, so project teams can see their own target dates and those of other projects.
- To reschedule, check the calendar first, then ask the COMMPUB EBCB **with a proposed new date** in mind.
- Make rescheduling requests **at least 2 days before** the original posting date.

### Blast times

You may propose other times as long as they follow the posting limits below.

:::times
- **Morning** · 10:00 AM
- **Afternoon** · 12:00 NN · 2:00 PM · 3:00 PM · 5:00 PM
- **Evening** · 6:00 PM · 7:00 PM · 8:00 PM
:::

- Put your preferred posting dates and times in the DRF. If you don't, COMMPUB will schedule each blast.
- Dates may shift if blasts from different projects conflict.

### Posting limits

**Timing**

1. Post on Ateneo Celadon channels **no earlier than 8 AM and no later than 10 PM**.
2. No two different posts go up at the same time, on any platform or project page.
3. Space posts **at least 2 hours apart** across all external Celadon platforms and pages.
4. Each project posts **1 output per day** (2 at most, for extreme exceptions).
5. The main Celadon Facebook page takes **at most 2 posts per day**, and all Celadon Facebook pages combined (including project pages) at most 3.

**Branding**

1. The official Celadon logo can't be used for personal posts. You may repost official deliverables (such as an event poster) and gimmicks (such as an Instagram bingo card) from personal accounts.
2. The Ateneo Celadon Facebook page is the main channel for every output. Anything posted there can also go on other platforms, but it doesn't have to. Posts and reels on other platforms must also go on Facebook. Instagram and TikTok *stories* are the exception.
3. Every output on the official Ateneo Celadon channels must first be approved by the relevant COMMPUB Managers.
4. The COMMPUB EBCB must have all of a post's details at least 1 day before it goes up.

### Branding guidelines for posting

**Digital Creatives**

- Every creative deliverable carries the Celadon logo. Public (external) materials use the **Dreagle** logo; internal materials, such as posts in the Celadon group, use the **Marquee** logo.
- Include the project logo.
- Include yearlong sponsors on every deliverable unless told otherwise.

**Videos**

- Every video ends with the Dreagle animation. Videos without it won't be posted.
- Include sponsor logos on sponsor-related videos.

**Photos**

- Event documentation photos are usually edited with a watermark or frame, which must show:
  - The Celadon logo (Dreagle for external projects, Marquee for internal ones)
  - The project logo
  - Yearlong and event sponsors
  - The event name
  - The photographer's name
- If no banner is provided for onsite coverage, COMMPUB uses the default Celadon watermark.

## Consultations

Consult the COMMPUB Managers and EBCB anytime about your outputs and project management, from the brandbook to onsite gimmick plans. A casual IC on Discord or Messenger works; message us in the core team chats, community chats, or the Manager Community Chat.

## Working well with COMMPUB core teams

1. **Communicate first.** Share your requests, context, output details, and deadlines before staffers start working.
2. **Be kind to your core team.** Check in, gauge their workload, and keep showing appreciation for their work.
3. **Check progress without making people feel unsafe.** Frame a bump as a progress check so timelines can adjust: "It's okay if it's not done yet — I just want to know the progress so we can adjust if needed."
4. **Encourage honest updates about delays.** Delays are manageable when flagged early, and PMs, core heads, COMMPUB Managers, and the EBCB can help find solutions.
5. **Work closely with the COMMPUB Managers.** They can guide task flow, clarify what staffers need, and help develop outputs. Give them room to be hands-on while respecting the project vision.
6. **Use each manager's expertise.** Production Design Managers can help Programs develop onsite gimmicks; Photos Managers can advise on DP shoot setup and lighting; Videos Managers can oversee onsite filming.
7. **Balance timely posting with staffer growth.** Follow up harder on outputs with fixed end or posting dates. Delays on less critical outputs are fine if they're communicated early.
8. **Don't overwork your core team.** Keep requests reasonable and give them ahead of time.
9. **Give revisions all at once** to avoid piecemeal edits and back-and-forth.
