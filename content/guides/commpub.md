## Core Team Management

### COMMPUB Committees Guide

Link: [CLDN Core Team Committee Roles Guide 2627](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1R8kzMhiiAjBp3W9wqUsi_l1z87PcpjkObgdiD_49LkM/edit)

### Core Team Head Roles and Responsibilities

1. To serve as the point person for both COMMPUB and Project Managers in their assigned project core team.
2. Lead their respective core team in delegating, planning, and executing tasks properly and punctually.
3. Ensure that all outputs from their respective core teams fit the standards and properly promote, as well as portray the project appropriately.
4. Work with their core teams to conceptualize ideas and ways to execute these.
5. Assist their core team members in the execution of their tasks.

### Deployment

1. COMMPUB Staffers may be deployed into Digital Creatives (DC), Documentation and Publications (DocPub), & Production Design (PD) Committees
2. Project Managers are expected to allot space for COMMPUB deployment in their core teams. As a working estimate, allot 1-2 slots for each COMMPUB pool involved in the project (i.e., 1-2 DC Staffers for the DC Committee; 1-2 Prod Design Staffers for the Prod Design Committee; 1 Videos, 1 Photos, and 1 Writing Staffer for the DocPub Committee)
3. Deployed COMMPUB Staffers do not need to go through the regular project application process (given that their pool matches the committee they wish to join). Once deployed, they are part of the Core Team.

### Core Team Group Chats

2. For cross-committee collaborations, create a dedicated GC/channel when needed, such as Photos x Production Design for DP Shoots or Photos x Digital Creatives for publications that involve photoshoots. Use the space to align task initiation, comments, and approval routing.
3. The COMMPUB EBCB point person will send a message in the GC/channel of each committee with the important links. Pin this message for easy access:

   2. To answer any questions by the project on COMMPUB-related matters.
   3. To monitor the quality of outputs produced by the COMMPUB core teams.
   4. To assist the COMMPUB core teams in planning and executing their outputs.
   5. To give comments and approval when asked by the core team.
   6. To guide the COMMPUB core team members, as well as their heads if necessary.
   7. To tag the COMMPUB EBCB representative when outputs are approved and ready for posting, when applicable.

## COMMPUB Deliverable Request Form

### Submitting Deliverable Requests BEFORE the beginning of the School Year

1. **Draft the Brandbook before finalizing the deliverables list in the DRF.**
   1. Open the [Brandbook Template](https://canva.link/lrz696trm3lkker) through the hyperlinked text in the DRF and follow the instructions in the Canva file. Project Managers may message COMMPUB EBCB for assistance throughout the process.
   2. The following contents are **REQUIRED** to be present in the Brandbook before proceeding to further steps. This must be filled out by the Project Manager before the start of the school year.
      1. MOOD BOARD slide
      2. WORD BANK slide that contains at least five (5) words that describe or relate to the proposed project theme. These will help Writing staffers align the tone of the spiels and help the rest of the core team get inspired when planning deliverables.
      3. DELIVERABLES slides (slides that detail the proposed deliverables for the project with pegs/references. Refer to the Deliverables Guide in the DRF to learn about common deliverable types per project)
   3. The following contents are OPTIONAL to be present in the Brandbook before proceeding to further steps:
      1. TYPOGRAPHY slide
      2. COLOR PALETTE slide
2. **Fill out the [Deliverable Request Form](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-myQOjynb_T34ezsn3F3IvdB49ORsINVvegFGLWbhYg/edit) (DRF).**
   1. Go to the tab named after your project. If your project's DRF tab is not yet available, you may create it by duplicating the template tab and customizing the copy accordingly.
   2. Fill out the Project/Initiative Details section.
   3. Read through the reminders thoroughly before filling out the other necessary details.
3. **Copy the Brandbook deliverable details into the DRF.**
   1. Transfer the proposed deliverable details into the DRF by filling out the Committee, Type, Target Blast Date, and Platform columns. See below for an example:
   2. Specify the exact platform/posting channel for each deliverable. If exact dates are not final yet, coordinate with COMMPUB EBCB for recommended timeline ranges or approximate posting weeks.
4. **Schedule the Brandbook + DRF meeting with COMMPUB EBCB**
   1. Upon completing the first draft of your Brandbook and DRF, notify at least one COMMPUB EBCB (i.e. @ in your project's DRF tab, send a message in Messenger, or @ in Manager Community Chat) so that they may start reviewing the draft before the scheduled consultation. *Note: You can also find COMMPUB EBCB and Manager contact details in the Welcome page of the DRF.*
   2. Book a timeslot in COMMPUB EBCB's Google Calendar Appointment System: [https://calendar.app.google/Dsgs6BqJbVvcawzLA](https://calendar.app.google/Dsgs6BqJbVvcawzLA)
   3. Target schedule: before the end of July
   4. Schedule follow-up meetings only if needed to finalize Typography, Color Palette, or Deliverable Pegs that were not finalized during the initial meeting.

## Spiel Masterfile

**What is the Spiel Masterfile?**

1. The [Spiel Masterfile](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1dg9EFX2ThorecILJwuhoENXPOg6zIzuyXpD1VyE_Jf8/edit) is where all captions for posts are written. Similar to the DRF, each project has its own tab.
2. Every social media output in the DRF must have a corresponding entry in the Spiel Masterfile, which should be filled out either by the Project Manager or the Core Head.
3. This is where the DocPub core team members assigned to writing spiels must input their outputs, and where the Writing Managers will approve and give comments to the spiels.

## COMMPUB Approval System and Deliverable Instructions

### General Approval Flow

:::flow
- Core Team Member
- Core Heads
- Project Managers
- COMMPUB Manager(s) of the relevant pool
- COMMPUB EBCB
:::

- Project Managers and Core Heads may give comments on outputs, but final approval for COMMPUB outputs comes from the relevant COMMPUB Manager(s).
- COMMPUB EBCB will mostly comment on readability, information accuracy, and other final details before posting.

1. The COMMPUB Managers are responsible for providing feedback and approval on the outputs that the project intends to post.
2. First drafts must be sent for approval at least 3 days before the proposed blast date. Core team members/Core Heads must tag the respective managers needed for approval.
   1. Ex: If the proposed blast date is September 15, the first draft must be sent to the relevant COMMPUB Managers by September 12, which is also considered the Internal Deadline (IDL).
3. Upon being tagged in a GC/channel for comments on an output draft, COMMPUB Managers generally have the next 12 hours to give their initial feedback. Managers are expected to give a transparency notice if they cannot respond with their comments within the 12 hours, along with the soonest possible time they can give their feedback.
4. Once an output is approved, the relevant COMMPUB Manager will mark the output as Approved and Ready for Posting in the DRF or Spiel Masterfile, and tag the COMMPUB EBCB representative. Outputs that are not approved by the relevant COMMPUB Manager(s) and COMMPUB EBCB will NOT be posted.

### Posting Limitations

**Post Timing**

1. Posts on all Ateneo Celadon social media channels shall be posted no earlier than 8am and no later than 10pm of that day.
2. No two unique posts shall be posted at the same time, regardless of social media platform or project page.
3. Posts should be at least 2 hours apart across all external Celadon platforms or pages.
4. Each project can only post 1 output per day (2 at most for extreme exceptions).
5. There can only be a maximum of 2 posts on the main Celadon Facebook page per day, a maximum of 3 posts across all Celadon Facebook pages (including project pages).

**Branding**

1. The official Celadon logo cannot be used for personal posts. However, you may repost official deliverables and outputs (e.g. event poster) and gimmicks (e.g. Instagram bingo card) via personal accounts.
2. The Ateneo Celadon Facebook page will be the main posting channel for all outputs. Whatever is posted on the Facebook page can also be posted on other platforms, but this is not required. Posts or reels posted to other social media platforms are required to also be posted on the Facebook page. However, Instagram or TikTok *stories* are allowed to be posted without them being posted to Facebook.
3. All outputs to be posted on the official Ateneo Celadon social media channels must be approved first by the respective COMMPUB managers.
4. The COMMPUB EBCB must have access to all post's details at least 1 day before the output is set to be posted.

## Tips for handling COMMPUB Core Teams

1. **Communication is key:** always relay your requests, context, output details, and deadlines before staffers start working.
2. **Please be nice to your core team!** A little kindness goes a long way, so please don't forget to check in with core teams, gauge their workload, and show constant appreciation for their work.
3. **Stay on top of progress checks without making core team members feel unsafe**. When bumping, frame it as a progress check so timelines can be adjusted if needed. For example: "It is okay if it is not done yet - I just want to know the progress so we can adjust if needed."
4. **Encourage honest updates about delays.** Remind core team members that delays can be handled if they communicate early. Project Managers, Core Heads, COMMPUB Managers, and EBCB can work with them to find solutions.
5. **Coordinate actively with COMMPUB Managers.** Managers can guide task flow, clarify what details staffers need, and help develop outputs. Give them space to be hands-on while still respecting the project vision.
6. **Use each manager's expertise.** Production Design Managers can help Programs develop onsite gimmicks and creative strategies. Photos Managers can advise on DP shoot setups and lighting. Video Managers may oversee onsite filming.
7. **Balance timely posting with staffer growth.** Identify outputs with crucial end dates or posting dates and be more proactive in following up on those. For less crucial outputs, delays are manageable as long as they are communicated early.
8. **Do not overwork or abuse core teams.** Requests should be reasonable and given ahead of time.
9. **Give revisions all at once as much as possible.** This helps avoid piecemeal edits and prevents unnecessary back-and-forth.
