## PM Checklist

### Pre-Project Requirements

1. **Core Team Applications (CTA)**
   - ☑ Core Team Request Form
   - ☑ Project Primer
   - ☑ Interview Sheet
   - ☑ Form Header
2. **Core Team Drive**

   Coordinate with your OSR Consultant deployed to your project (if none, please coordinate with the OSR EBCB) to assist in managing sharing permissions and file management.

### Post-Project Requirements

1. **Post-Project Evaluations**
   - ☑ Core Team Post-Project Evaluation (PPE)
   - ☑ Participant Evaluations
2. **Sustainability Report**

   Anticipate and coordinate with the deployed OSR Consultant for the Core Team debriefing session. Expect the Consultants to conduct individual consultations (ICs) with you and the committee heads as well.

## Core Team Applications (CTA)

### Formation Process

:::steps
1. The OSR EBCB will provide you with your project's respective **CTA Project Folder**.
   - In the folder, you will also be provided with the following files to edit and use:
     1. Applications Interview Form (GForm to input the answers of interviewed applicants)
     2. CTA Decision Emails Template (GDoc)
2. Specific and comprehensive instructions (GDocs) on how to accomplish various deliverables will be provided for each wave your project is a part of. These deliverables include:
   1. Answering the Core Team Request Form
   2. Making your Core Team Interview Questionnaire
   3. Adding your availabilities for Core Team Interviews

   *NOTE: Instructions may slightly vary for each wave. Expect these instructions to be disseminated via the Manager Community Chat (Messenger) during the wave/s your project is under.*

   - Accomplish **ALL DELIVERABLES** before proceeding to conduct applicant interviews.
3. General Interview Process & Instructions
   1. Interview Sheet:
      1. Make a copy of the template tab in the designated Interview Sheet (GSheet) for your project. Feel free to customize the aesthetics of the tab. However,
      2. Indicate your availability for interviews in the Interview Sheets.

      *NOTE: Each wave will have a separate Interview Sheet that will be disseminated by OSR. Additionally, for projects included in Wave 1 onwards, refrain from merging cells or adding new cells to prevent any possible errors with the AppScript code.*
   2. Interview Questionnaire:
      - Edit the Application Interview GForm provided in your project's CTA folder with the spiels, questions, etc. that you use for the interviews. The following sections are typically included in the interviews:
        1. General Questions
        2. Leadership Questions
        3. Position and committee-specific questions (Core Heads are to be asked a different set of questions)
        4. Fun Questions
   3. CT applicants will book their interview schedule after submitting the application GForm. As an interviewer, confirm the interview details with the interviewee through either Messenger (highly encouraged) or email.

      *NOTE: For Waves 0 and 0.5, keep yourself regularly updated with the interviews booked in the Interview Sheet. For Wave 1 onwards, the interviewee and selected interviewer will receive an email notifying them of the booking details.*
   4. During the interview, input the interviewee's answers through the Application Interview GForm.
4. Deliberations:
   1. The answers of an applicant in the application GForm and during the interview can be seen in the automated Applicant Tracker Dashboard prepared by OSR.
   2. Fill up your project's designated Wave Results GSheet with your final list of accepted and rejected members.
   3. After the GSheet is accomplished, notify the OSR EBCB. We will send the decision emails to accepted and rejected applicants, together with the decision emails for the other projects included in the Wave.

   *NOTE: Should CT members of your project be part of another CT whose working timeline overlaps with yours, the OSR EBCB will coordinate with the concerned member and or PMs to prevent potential burnout.*
5. The final list of CT members following the wave applications will be posted in the internal Facebook Group.
6. Create all the communication channels for your project CT (Messenger or Telegram).
   - You are HIGHLY ENCOURAGED to facilitate communication through Messenger Community Chat since most members are best reached through Messenger.
     - Ideally, minimize the creation of multiple and separate GCs to prevent confusion and other issues (remind your members of this, as well). Maximize the Manager Community Chat in communicating with the EBCB. As PM, you are responsible for ensuring that you have proper tracking and oversight of your project.
   - Include the department EBCB of the project in pertinent communication channels.
:::

## OSR Deployments

### OSR Consultants (Managers)

- All projects will have an assigned OSR Consultant. Should there be no assigned consultant or the consultant does not fulfill their responsibilities, the OSR EBCB will be present for direct coordination with the project.
  - For projects with Operations or Recruitment & Secretariat committees, the assigned consultant will be deployed as its CT head.

*NOTE: You may still get another core team head for the committee, as needed.*

- The consultant is responsible for assisting PMs in managing OSR-related systems and data, including but not limited to:
  - Core Team Applications (CTA)
  - Project masterfiles, drives, and files
  - OSR requests *(e.g., email blasts and file requests)*
- They are also responsible for the following:
  - Facilitating mid-project and post-project ICs
  - Facilitating the post-project debriefing session with CT heads and PMs.
  - Leading the completion of OSR's sustainability report for the project.

### OSR Junior Analysts (Deputies)

- For projects with Operations or Recruitment & Secretariat committees, OSR will deploy **one to two deputies** to these committees.
- They will assist the OSR Consultants in their role, particularly in the facilitation of ICs and completion of the sustainability report. They will function as normal core team members with the same responsibilities as other non-deployed members of their committee.

*NOTE: Junior Analysts will be deployed only in Wave 1 onwards.*

## Post-Project Evaluations

### Participant Evaluations

- Participant evaluations, which are to be made by the assigned/deployed OSR Consultant and Junior Analyst/s, may be done through the following methods:
  1. Google Forms with a shortened link (tinyurl/bit.ly) and QR Code
  2. On-site/physical gimmicks (e.g., written feedback, feedback boards, tally jars)
- Participant evaluation forms must be included and integrated into the slide deck or program proper of projects, ideally towards the end. For GForms, the responder link must also be ideally sent to participants after the event ends, possibly via email or other communication channels. There must be a MINIMUM response rate of AT LEAST 50% of the total number of participants.

### Core Team Evaluations

- CT Evaluations, also led by the OSR Consultant, will and should be conducted immediately after the execution phase of a project. Internal feedback may be collected through the following methods:
  1. Google Forms
  2. Live Debriefing Session (with core heads)
  3. Individual Consultations

### Sustainability Reports

- These reports will be accomplished by the assigned OSR Consultant and Junior Analysts for your project. As PMs, you are to aid the completion of the report by providing information through ICs (mid-project and post-project) and answering other questions OSR may ask.
- The report should ideally be completed 1-1.5 months after the execution of the project. Should time permit, the completed report will be presented to you and the pertinent EBCB after the project.

## OSR Research Initiative

- **Research Initiative**
  - A research initiative is a way for you to obtain any information you may want to know about the Celadon or Ateneo community.
  - Some sample information includes: common Atenean tambay areas, free times of Celadoneans, etc.
- **Research Initiative Process**

:::steps
1. Consult your OSR Consultant or the OSR EBCB to align on:
   1. Purpose of Research Initiative
   2. Types of Data Collection
      - Google Forms
      - Focus Group Discussions
      - Social Media Polls (e.g., Social media stories, posts, polls)
      - Onsite Feedback (e.g., Tally, feedback boards, smiley meters)
      - Micro-Interviews (e.g., Casual, flash interviews)
   3. List of questions to ask/ data points needed
   4. Target completion date

   NOTE: Unless confidential, coordinate with the OSR Consultant or EBCB through the Manager Community Chat.
2. The consultant will coordinate with OSR EBCB (and COMMPUB for social media/on-ground executions) for approval.
3. Once approved, the OSR Consultant and or Junior Analysts will carry out the initiative.
:::

## OSR Centralized Request Form (CRF)

### Data Requests

- **Possible Data that can be requested**
  - Past Project Files
    - Project Proposal Form {PPF}
    - Mid-Project Report
    - Sustainability Report
  - Past Core Team Google Drives
  - Etc.
- **Process for Requesting**

:::steps
1. Fill out [OSR's Centralized Request Form](https://forms.gle/z1corsV7nVbuesk46) **AT LEAST 2 DAYS** before the data is needed. The following details are requested from you:
   1. Student and Contact Information
   2. Department
   3. Data/File Requested
   4. Reason for request
   5. Date needed by
2. Upon submitting the request, send a message to [Kyle Co](https://m.me/Kyledominic.co) or [Sofia Diño](https://m.me/sofiag.dino) via Messenger to inform them of the request. Wait for the request to be acknowledged and processed.
3. If approved, the files will be shared with your Ateneo Email. If rejected or there are other concerns, the OSR EBCB will inform you via Messenger or Gmail.
:::

- **Things to Note**
  - Depending on the sensitivity of the data you're requesting, the OSR EBCB may reject the request. Other details in these files or data may also be redacted.
  - You are only given access to the files you've requested for the duration of your project. Your access will then be revoked after the project.
  - Certain iterations of Past Project Google Drives, sustainability reports, and other relevant files for certain projects do not exist.
  - Due to the discontinuation of OBF emails, several files have been lost. So, ensure to consult with your department EBCB and or the OSR EBCB for important concerns and questions.

### Email Blasts

- **Process for Requesting**

:::steps
1. Fill out [OSR's Centralized Request Form](https://forms.gle/z1corsV7nVbuesk46) **AT LEAST 2 DAYS** before you want the email to be blasted. The following details are requested from you:
   1. Student and Contact Information
   2. Department
   3. Type of Email Blast (Blast or follow-up)
   4. Target Recipients

      Possible Recipients
      1. Undergraduate Students
      2. All Celadon Members
      3. Specific batches of undergraduate students
   5. Subject Line
   6. Target Date and Time
   7. Body
   8. Other notes

   NOTE: You may refer to the following samples as a reference for how the email blast request may look and is requested for. These references are also linked within the CRF.
   - [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1HhteUzVmmad73LUhEXvqbm0zVHBy6nsWXqbFCxgKTiY/edit) (regular text, with layout, HTML)
   - [Canva](https://canva.link/e6db72w14yv2ot6)
   - [Google Email Layout](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1SZ1ZjL06nBfnrsM1-EhIm2X7ekR_ui2kzabS0hH5FL8/view)
2. Upon submitting the request, send a message to [Kyle Co](https://m.me/Kyledominic.co) or [Lyss Orquina](https://m.me/alyssa.orquina) via Messenger to inform them of the request. Wait for the request to be acknowledged and processed.
3. The OSR EBCB will inform you if the email blast request is approved, rejected, or if there are any other concerns, via Messenger or Gmail
:::

- **Things to Note**
  - Should your scheduled blast coincide with other major blasts, regardless of target recipients, you will immediately be contacted by OSR to agree upon another blasting schedule. To avoid this, you may consult with the relevant OSR EBCB before submitting your request.
  - You are only allowed to submit one type of blast per request.
  - Any edits done after submitting the form, regardless of approval must be IMMEDIATELY NOTIFIED TO RELEVANT OSR EBCB
  - Revisions are only allowed until FIVE HOURS BEFORE the Blast Schedule.

### FUSION (Facilitating Unified Systems, Insights, and Organizational Networks)

- Departments may request technical and research support from their designated OSR liaison team. Requests may be either of the following:
  1. Systems Support: Build and maintain efficient systems.
     1. Scope: Creation, optimization, and automation of Google Sheets, forms, trackers, and master databases.
  2. Applied Research: Conduct targeted research initiatives and analyze old or unused data.
     1. Scope: Targeted studies and analysis of archived or new datasets (e.g., member engagement, social media trends, or partnership value)
- To process FUSION requests, fill out the designated section for system through OSR's Centralized Request Form. Please read through the instructions linked in the GForm.
