## PM checklist

:::compare
**Before the project**

- **Core Team Applications (CTA)**
  - ☐ Core Team Request Form
  - ☐ Project primer
  - ☐ Interview sheet
  - ☐ Form header
- **Core team drive.** Work with your deployed OSR Consultant (or the OSR EBCB if you have none) on sharing permissions and file management.

**After the project**

- **Post-project evaluations**
  - ☐ Core team post-project evaluation (PPE)
  - ☐ Participant evaluations

## Core team applications (CTA)

### Forming your core team

:::steps
1. **Get your CTA project folder from the OSR EBCB.** It contains an Applications Interview Form (a Google Form for recording interview answers) and a CTA decision emails template (a Google Doc).
2. **Complete every wave deliverable before interviewing.** For each wave your project is in, OSR shares detailed instructions in the Manager Community Chat on Messenger, covering the Core Team Request Form, your interview questionnaire, and your interview availability. Instructions may differ slightly per wave.
3. **Set up the interview sheet.** Copy the template tab in your wave's Interview Sheet for your project. You may restyle the tab, but from Wave 1 onwards don't merge or add cells, because the Apps Script that runs the sheet depends on its structure. Add your interview availability there. Each wave has its own Interview Sheet from OSR.
4. **Write your interview questionnaire.** Edit the Application Interview Google Form in your CTA folder with your spiels and questions. Interviews usually cover general questions, leadership questions, position- and committee-specific questions (core heads get a different set), and fun questions.
5. **Confirm interviews.** Applicants book a slot after submitting the application form. Confirm the details with them on Messenger (preferred) or by email. In Waves 0 and 0.5, keep checking the Interview Sheet for bookings; from Wave 1, the applicant and interviewer both get a booking email.
6. **Interview.** Enter the applicant's answers in the Application Interview Google Form as you go.
7. **Deliberate.** Each applicant's form and interview answers appear in OSR's Applicant Tracker Dashboard. Fill in your project's Wave Results sheet with your accepted and rejected applicants, then notify the OSR EBCB. OSR sends the decision emails for every project in the wave together.
8. **Welcome the team.** The final core team list is posted in the internal Facebook group. Create your project's communication channels.
:::

:::note
If a member of your core team is also on another core team with an overlapping timeline, the OSR EBCB will coordinate with them and the PMs to prevent burnout.
:::

**Communication channels**

- Use a **Messenger community chat** if you can; most members are easiest to reach on Messenger. Telegram also works.
- Keep separate group chats to a minimum to avoid confusion, and remind your members to do the same. Use the Manager Community Chat to reach the EBCB. As PM, you're responsible for keeping track of your project.
- Add your project's department EBCB to the relevant channels.

## OSR deployments

:::compare
**OSR Consultants (Managers)**

Every project gets an OSR Consultant. If there isn't one, or they aren't fulfilling their role, the OSR EBCB coordinates with the project directly. For projects with an Operations or Recruitment & Secretariat committee, the consultant is deployed as that committee's head (you may still add another head if needed).

The consultant helps PMs with OSR systems and data:

- Core team applications
- Project masterfiles, drives, and files
- OSR requests, such as email blasts and file requests

They also:

- Facilitate mid-project and post-project ICs
- Facilitate the post-project debriefing with core heads and PMs
- Lead the project's sustainability report

**OSR Junior Analysts (Deputies)**

For projects with an Operations or Recruitment & Secretariat committee, OSR deploys **one to two deputies** to that committee, from Wave 1 onwards.

They help the OSR Consultant run ICs and complete the sustainability report, and otherwise work as regular core team members with the same responsibilities as the rest of their committee.
:::

## Post-project evaluations

### Participant evaluations

The deployed OSR Consultant and Junior Analysts make the participant evaluation, using:

1. A Google Form with a short link (TinyURL or bit.ly) and a QR code
2. Onsite gimmicks such as written feedback, feedback boards, and tally jars

Build the evaluation into the slide deck or program, ideally near the end. For Google Forms, also send the link to participants after the event, by email or another channel. **Aim for a response rate of at least 50% of participants.**

### Core team evaluations

The OSR Consultant runs core team evaluations right after the execution phase, through:

1. Google Forms
2. A live debriefing session with the core heads
3. Individual consultations

### Sustainability reports

The OSR Consultant and Junior Analysts write the report. PMs help by sharing information in the mid-project and post-project ICs and answering OSR's questions. Reports are ideally done **1 to 1.5 months after the project**, and when there's time, they're presented to you and the relevant EBCB.

## Research initiatives

A research initiative gets you information about the Celadon or Ateneo community, such as the most common Atenean tambay spots or when Celadoneans are free.

:::steps
1. **Align with your OSR Consultant or the OSR EBCB** on the purpose, the data collection method (Google Forms, focus group discussions, social media polls, onsite feedback such as tallies, feedback boards, or smiley meters, or quick micro-interviews), the questions or data points you need, and the target completion date. Unless it's confidential, coordinate through the Manager Community Chat.
2. **Approval.** The consultant gets approval from the OSR EBCB, and from COMMPUB for anything on social media or on the ground.
3. **Run it.** Once approved, the OSR Consultant and Junior Analysts carry out the initiative.
:::

## OSR Centralized Request Form (CRF)

:::links
- [OSR Centralized Request Form](https://forms.gle/z1corsV7nVbuesk46) — for data requests, email blasts, and FUSION support.
:::

### Data requests

You can request past project files (PPFs, mid-project reports, sustainability reports), past core team Google Drives, and similar records.

:::steps
1. **Submit the CRF at least 2 days before you need the data.** It asks for your contact information, department, the data or file you need, why, and the date you need it by.
2. **Message [Kyle Co](https://m.me/Kyledominic.co) or [Sofia Diño](https://m.me/sofiag.dino) on Messenger** to let them know, and wait for them to acknowledge and process the request.
3. **Get the files.** If approved, they're shared with your Ateneo email. If the request is rejected or there's an issue, the OSR EBCB tells you on Messenger or by email.
:::

:::note
- The OSR EBCB may reject sensitive requests or redact parts of the files.
- You have access only for the duration of your project; it's revoked afterwards.
- Some past project drives, sustainability reports, and other files don't exist for certain projects. Several were lost when the OBF emails were discontinued, so check with your department EBCB or the OSR EBCB for anything important.
:::

### Email blasts

:::steps
1. **Submit the CRF at least 2 days before the blast.** It asks for your contact information, department, blast type (new blast or follow-up), recipients (all undergraduates, all Celadon members, or specific undergraduate batches), subject line, target date and time, body, and any other notes.
2. **Use a sample as reference:** a [Google Docs version](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1HhteUzVmmad73LUhEXvqbm0zVHBy6nsWXqbFCxgKTiY/edit) (plain text, with layout, or HTML), a [Canva version](https://canva.link/e6db72w14yv2ot6), or a [Google email layout](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1SZ1ZjL06nBfnrsM1-EhIm2X7ekR_ui2kzabS0hH5FL8/view). These are also linked in the CRF.
3. **Message [Kyle Co](https://m.me/Kyledominic.co) or [Lyss Orquina](https://m.me/alyssa.orquina) on Messenger** and wait for them to acknowledge and process the request.
4. **Wait for the decision.** The OSR EBCB tells you on Messenger or by email whether the blast is approved, rejected, or needs changes.
:::

:::note
- If your blast clashes with another major blast, whatever the recipients, OSR will contact you to agree on a new time. Consult the OSR EBCB before submitting to avoid this.
- One blast type per request.
- Tell the OSR EBCB **immediately** about any edit after submitting, approved or not.
- Revisions are allowed until **5 hours before** the scheduled blast.
:::

### FUSION

FUSION (Facilitating Unified Systems, Insights, and Organizational Networks) gives departments technical and research support from their OSR liaison team:

:::compare
**Systems support**

Building and maintaining efficient systems: creating, optimizing, and automating Google Sheets, forms, trackers, and master databases.

**Applied research**

Targeted research and analysis of archived or new data, such as member engagement, social media trends, or partnership value.
:::

To request FUSION support, fill out its section of the [Centralized Request Form](https://forms.gle/z1corsV7nVbuesk46) and read the instructions linked in the form.
