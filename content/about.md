## Our vision

Ateneo Celadon envisions a community that is **deeply connected to Chinese-Filipino culture** and is committed to engaging in its **identity, appreciation, and discourse** within the greater Philippine society.

## Our mission

Ateneo Celadon strives to engage its members and audiences in **learning, experiencing, and facilitating exchange between the Chinese-Filipino culture** and greater Philippine society through culturally rooted efforts. In line with this, the organization aims to **develop member relations and equip them with different skills** that are relevant to the practice and discourse of Chinese-Filipino culture.

## Our core advocacy

:::pull
To foster nationhood through culture-sharing and discourse.
:::

## Thrust of the year

Ateneo Celadon aims to refocus the organization's vision and mission, **unite departments and initiatives** under a common goal, and **develop the organization's future leaders** for the active Celadonean sharing their heritage, the floating Celadonean looking for a home, and the Atenean meeting the cultures woven into society, toward the **discourse, appreciation, and cultivation of Chinese-Filipino culture**.

## Core competencies

1. **Chinese-Filipino values** rooted in openness, sensitivity, excellence, and family.
2. A shared responsibility to understand, advocate for, and integrate **Chinese-Filipino socio-cultural heritage**.
